# 002 — Style Decisions & Terminology

This document establishes the literary register, typographic rules, and canonical Persian terminology for the translation of *Lao-tzu's Taoteching*.

---

## 1. Register Architecture: Three Voices in One Book

*Lao-tzu's Taoteching* is not a monolithic text. Each chapter contains three distinct registers that must be clearly distinguished:

### 1. The Poetic Verses (متن اشعار دائو د جینگ)
- **Voice**: Terse, paradoxical, rhythmically measured, and contemplative.
- **Rules**:
  - Do not flatten poetry into explanatory narrative prose.
  - Maintain short, self-contained poetic lines.
  - Avoid heavy, ornate Arabicized scholastic jargon; prefer direct, pure Persian words where possible.
  - Avoid conversational colloquialisms.

### 2. The Classical Commentaries (تفسیرهای کلاسیک)
- **Voice**: Formal, reflective philosophical exposition by Tang, Song, and Ming dynasty masters.
- **Rules**:
  - Always introduce commentators using the standard formula: `**[نام مفسر]** می‌گوید:`
  - Maintain formal, scholarly Persian syntax.
  - Ensure all direct quotes from sutras, Confucius, or historical annals are framed in Persian guillemets (`«...»`).

### 3. Red Pine's Textual Apparatus (یادداشت‌های پژوهشی رِد پاین)
- **Voice**: Contemporary, objective, analytical sinological criticism.
- **Rules**:
  - Preserves precise scholarly references to archaeological discoveries:
    - *Mawangtui silk texts* → دست‌نوشته‌های ابریشمی ماوانگ‌دوی
    - *Kuotien bamboo slips* → باریکه‌های بامبوی گوادیان (گوتیِن)
    - *Fuyi edition* → نسخه فو یی
    - *Wang Pi edition* → نسخه وانگ پی
    - *Shuowen dictionary* → لغت‌نامه شوئو ون

## 4. Typography & Orthography Standards

1. **Quotation Marks**:
   - Always use Persian guillemets: `«...»`.
   - Never leave raw English quotes (`"..."`, `“...”`).
2. **Numbers & Digits**:
   - Verse numbers, footnotes, and dates in Persian text use Persian-Indic digits (`۰ ۱ ۲ ۳ ۴ ۵ ۶ ۷ ۸ ۹`).
3. **Zero-Width Non-Joiner (ZWNJ / نیم‌فاصله)**:
   - Mandatory for prefixes: `می‌شود` (نه `میشود` یا `می شود`).
   - Mandatory for plural suffixes: `کتاب‌ها` (نه `کتابها`).
   - Mandatory for compounds: `بی‌عملی`، `دست‌نوشته`.
4. **Persian Characters**:
   - Always Persian `ی` (U+06CC) and `ک` (U+06A9), never Arabic `ي` (U+064A) or `ك` (U+0643).
